# Privacy Policy - Autopost Syucai

Autopost Syucai is a personal tool used by its owner, Alexander Chernov, to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channel, Instagram and VK community.

- It runs only on the owner's computer and signs in only to the owner's own accounts
- YouTube access is used only to upload the owner's videos and read the owner's channel name

- Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
